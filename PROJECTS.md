### AQUA-ETHIC · Water quality monitoring

A four-person DECODE SIH hackathon project. I contributed to the system that validates ESP32 readings, stores them through an Express API, and hashes them so recorded measurements can be checked for tampering. It supports Solidity blockchain anchoring and a local verification simulator.

`ESP32` · `Node.js` · `Express` · `SQLite` · `Solidity`\
[Source code](https://github.com/ANTOJERRIN/AQUA-ETHIC) · [Demo](https://aqua-ethic.onrender.com/)

### Lifeline · Medical records platform

`React` · `FastAPI` · `Supabase` · `OCR`\
[Source code](https://github.com/ANTOJERRIN/LIFELINE-) · [Demo](https://lifeline-17u5.onrender.com/)

`Flutter` · `Firebase` · `Gemini` · `Nearby Connections`\
